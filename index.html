Privacy Policy & Price Policy
Effective date: October 9, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to DineNDeal ("we", "us", "our"). DineNDeal is a mobile application that helps users discover restaurants on a map and browse detailed restaurant listings, menus, deals, and reviews. This document outlines our Privacy Policy (how we handle user data) and our Price Policy (how restaurant listings and pricing information are displayed).

2. Privacy Policy

2.1 No personal data collection

DineNDeal does not collect, store, or process any personal information from users. The app functions without requiring login, registration, or any form of data submission.

2.2 Location data

The app may request temporary access to your device’s location only to show nearby restaurants on the map. This information is used locally on your device and is not stored or transmitted to any server by us.

2.3 Anonymous usage

DineNDeal operates fully anonymously. No name, email, contact details, or identifiers are collected. We do not use cookies, tracking, or analytics that store identifiable user data.

2.4 Third-party services

The app uses third-party map services (such as Google Maps API) to display restaurant locations. These third-party services may collect limited technical information as per their own privacy policies. We do not control or store this data.

2.5 Security

Since no personal data is collected or stored, there is minimal privacy risk. We maintain general app security best practices to ensure a safe experience.

2.6 Children’s privacy

DineNDeal does not collect information from anyone, including children. The app is safe for general audiences.
